# PE16 claim card

Read scope: Richard Paul and Linda Elder, *The Thinker's Guide to Socratic Questioning*, public
preview pages 4–8, 59, 70, and 93.

Supported claims:

- Socratic questioning requires systematic depth and follow-up responsive to context.
- Question categories are possible directions, not a mechanical sequence.
- Listening closely to an answer is necessary to choose the next useful question.
- The educational aim includes intellectual autonomy rather than passive acceptance.

Limits:

- The passage does not establish that completing a taxonomy constitutes inquiry.
- The passage does not study AI dialogue or establish that it causes mastery.
