# P93 claim card

Read scope: Christine Padesky, “Socratic Questioning: Changing Minds or Guiding Discovery?”,
pages 1–6.

Supported claims:

- Guided discovery depends on genuine curiosity, listening, summaries, and concrete information.
- The learner, not the questioner, draws the synthesis.
- Questions aimed at a predetermined conclusion are covert persuasion rather than guided discovery.

Limits:

- This card does not authorize psychological diagnosis or therapy.
- It contains no evidence about AI-specific educational efficacy.

Source: https://padesky.com/wp-content/uploads/2012/11/socquest.pdf
