# KPU19 claim card

Read scope: Kwantlen Polytechnic University, *Level Two Peer Tutoring Fundamentals and
Integration Workbook*, chapter 7.

Supported claims:

- Questioning should support independent thinking rather than supply the answer.
- An ineffective question can be rephrased while leaving the learner responsible for the move.

Limits:

- The chapter does not validate a universal question sequence.
- It does not provide an automatic measure of mastery.

Source: https://kpu.pressbooks.pub/leveltwopeertutoringfundamentals/chapter/use-socratic-questions/
